From Peak to Published Short

Guide · AuraFlux

Peaks starts from moments the platforms already chart: YouTube Most Replayed, Twitch chat spikes, and Kick concurrent-viewer peaks. You pick the window, stage a trim, and the AuraFlux pipeline produces a Short.

In the member portal

Open Peaks, choose the source, run analysis, stage the clip, select a compose preset (default C9 Speed), and submit the job. Review and publish to connected accounts when the pipeline marks compliant.

Why it matters

Editors used to be the capacity bottleneck — not just cost per video. Peaks replaces “scrub the VOD hoping for a moment” with signal-first production so you can ship more Shorts without growing headcount.
