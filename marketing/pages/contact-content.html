What's included in the AuraFlux plan?
Unlimited Peaks (Most Replayed, chat spikes, CCV), Short production, and publish for one brand, subject to fair-use rate limits in the Terms. Inquire about Managed if you want done-for-you production.
Is usage really unlimited?
Yes for normal Peaks-to-publish use on one brand. To keep the platform reliable we rate-limit job starts, concurrent jobs, and Peaks analyses per hour. Current numbers are in the Terms of Service.
Can I connect multiple social accounts?
Yes - connect all major platforms and target any job to one or more simultaneously.
