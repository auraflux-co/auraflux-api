The Production Pipeline

Six portals. Each one checks the work before the next begins.

P0

Content Intelligence

Source video analysis, segment detection, and clip scoring. The system identifies which moments are worth producing before any editing begins. Supports YouTube, Twitch, Kick replays, and direct uploads.

Always On
P1

Script & Voice

Script tailored to your content type and platform format. ElevenLabs TTS voiceover produced at broadcast quality. Skip this portal entirely if you want to use your own audio.

Optional
P2

Edit & Assembly

Auto-cut, clip merging, vertical crop and reframe for mobile formats, audio loudness normalization. This is where the raw source becomes a finished video: paced, structured, and platform-ready.

Always On
P3

Quality Assurance

Two-layer QA: FFmpeg defect detection checks for frozen frames, silence gaps, and encoding issues. Creative QA scores the output for watchability. Jobs that don't meet the quality threshold are flagged before proceeding.

QA Gate
P4

Brand & Effects

Lower thirds with segment labels, auto-generated thumbnails, scene transitions, YouTube chapter markers, and speaker captions. Every feature is opt-in. Select exactly what your content needs when you create the job.

Configurable
P5

Publish

Direct publish to YouTube and TikTok via OAuth. Title, description, tags, thumbnail, chapter markers, and scheduling all applied automatically. No manual export. No upload queue. Done.
