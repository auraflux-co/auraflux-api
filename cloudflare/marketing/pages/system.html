The Production Pipeline

Six portals. Each one checks the work before the next begins.

P0

Content Intelligence

Source video analysis, segment detection, and clip scoring. The system identifies which moments are worth producing before any editing begins. Supports YouTube URLs, Twitch VODs, Kick replays, and direct uploads.

Always On
↓
P1

Script & Voice

AI-generated script tailored to your content type and platform format. ElevenLabs TTS voiceover produced at broadcast quality. Skip this portal entirely if you want to use your own audio.

Optional
↓
P2

Edit & Assembly

Auto-cut, clip merging, vertical crop and reframe for mobile formats, audio loudness normalization. This is where the raw source becomes a finished video — paced, structured, and platform-ready.

Always On
↓
P3

Quality Assurance

Two-layer QA: FFmpeg defect detection checks for frozen frames, silence gaps, and encoding issues. Creative QA scores the output for watchability. Jobs that don't meet the quality threshold are flagged before proceeding.

QA Gate
↓
P4

Brand & Effects

Lower thirds with segment labels, AI-generated thumbnails, scene transitions, YouTube chapter markers, and speaker captions. Every feature is opt-in — select exactly what your content needs when you create the job.

Configurable
↓
P5

Publish

Direct publish to YouTube and TikTok via OAuth. Title, description, tags, thumbnail, chapter markers, and scheduling all applied automatically. No manual export. No upload queue. Done.

What You Can Produce

Four content formats, each with its own production logic.

🎮

Highlight Clips

Best-moment extraction from gaming streams, sports broadcasts, or live events. Scored by engagement density, audio peaks, and crowd reaction. Outputs multiple clip options or one optimized single.

🎤

Gameplay Commentary

Turn a full gaming session or VOD into a structured commentary video with AI narration, auto-paced cuts, and platform-specific framing. Great for YouTube long-form.

🎬

Show & Film Analysis

Source a TV show, film, or documentary clip. AuraFlux generates commentary, assembles reaction-style content, and packages it for publish — all within fair-use clip lengths.

📡

Live Event Recap

Post-event summary video from a live stream or event recording. Automatically segments the timeline, selects key moments, and produces a short-form recap optimized for social.
