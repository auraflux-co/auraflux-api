Why We Built This

Most streamers and creators produce hours of content every week — and then lose most of it. Editing takes longer than the stream itself. Hiring an editor costs more than the content earns. So the clips sit on a hard drive, and the channel doesn't grow.

We built AuraFlux to close that gap. Not by making editing faster, but by eliminating the need for it entirely. Our platform takes your raw stream or uploaded video, runs it through an AI-powered production pipeline, and delivers polished, platform-ready content — automatically.

"Every creator should be able to produce like a media company. That's not a staffing problem — it's a software problem."

AuraFlux is the software answer to that problem. The same pipeline that a five-person content team would run — clip selection, scripting, voiceover, assembly, quality checks, brand application, and direct publish — happens automatically, for every piece of content you produce.

What Makes AuraFlux Different

Not a tool. An automated production environment.

⚙

A full pipeline, not a feature

Other tools do one thing — trim clips, generate captions, schedule posts. AuraFlux does all of it, in sequence, automatically. One job submission runs the entire production process end to end.

🎯

Platform-aware by default

Every job knows its destination. Vertical crop for TikTok. Chapter markers for YouTube. Captions calibrated for each platform's viewer behavior. No manual reformatting.

🔍

AI quality checks at every stage

Each portal runs QA before passing to the next. Frozen frames, silence gaps, assembly defects, and creative quality are all checked before your content goes live.

📡

Direct publish, no manual export

Connect your YouTube and TikTok accounts once. AuraFlux publishes directly via OAuth — with your title, description, tags, thumbnail, and chapter markers already applied.

💬

Collab guides you through it

Every plan includes Collab, the in-app AI assistant. Ask it anything — what features are available, how credits work, what to do next. It knows the full platform and responds in plain English.

🔌

API-first for technical operators

The entire platform is accessible via REST API. Integrate AuraFlux directly into your own tooling, dashboard, or workflow automation. Full developer documentation included.

The Technology Behind the Platform

Six production portals. One automated pipeline.

0

Content Intelligence

Source video analysis — segment detection, clip scoring, content type classification. The pipeline decides which moments are worth producing before any editing begins.

1

Script & Voice

AI script generation tailored to your content type and format. ElevenLabs TTS voiceover production in your brand voice. Optional — skip it if you want your own audio.

2

Edit & Assembly

Auto-cut, clip merging, vertical crop and reframe for mobile formats, audio normalization. This is where raw content becomes a watchable video.

3

Quality Assurance

FFmpeg defect detection (freeze frames, silence gaps, encoding issues) followed by creative QA scoring. Jobs that don't meet the quality bar are flagged for review before they proceed.

4

Brand & Effects

Lower thirds, AI-generated thumbnails, scene transitions, chapter markers, captions. Your brand applied consistently to every piece of content, at scale.

5

Publish

Direct publish to YouTube and TikTok via OAuth. Title, description, tags, thumbnails, and chapter markers all applied automatically. One job. Done.
