FAQ

Common Questions

What's the difference between our plans?
Both plans include the full AuraFlux automated engine. Operate is entirely self-serve via our dashboard and API. Guided integrates our Gemini-backed Collab AI assistant to monitor and refine your outputs. Managed adds dedicated human account managers alongside the AI layer.
How do credits work?
Each plan includes a monthly credit allowance. One credit = one complete production job. Unused credits roll over automatically, and Extra Credit Packs can be purchased any time directly inside your account dashboard.
Can I connect multiple social accounts?
Yes — connect all major platforms and target any job to one or more simultaneously.
